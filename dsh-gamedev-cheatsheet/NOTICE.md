# NOTICE

## Upstream source

The bundled skill (`skill/`) adapts material from **Game Dev Cheat Sheet**,
<https://gamedev-cheatsheet.com>, by **Nicolas Zullo** (@NicolasZu).

From that source come, per concept:

- the concept list and its four-layer grouping;
- the short English "prompt bit" prompts;
- the "when to use" notes and the engine-neutral pseudocode sketches;
- the "common mistakes" notes and the related-concept links.

Everything else in the skill — the Chinese explanations, the task/symptom
routing table, the default-values table, the twelve common-mistakes list, the
pre-ship checklists, the engine mapping — was written for this package.
